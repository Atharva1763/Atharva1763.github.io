I'm a **Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India**, where I focus on **LLM inference optimization**, **congestion control**, and **sustainable AI inference**. I'm a core contributor to **AI-Greenferencing** — Microsoft's effort on power-, hardware-, and workload-aware routing for renewable-energy-powered AI inference clusters.

My research lives at the intersection of **LLM systems**, **NLP**, and **applied ML**. Recent work includes the **XWind** cross-site router for LLM inference at renewable-energy farms (ASPLOS 2027, under review) and **BeLLMan** on LLM congestion control ([FAISys 2025](#)); I've also published at [ACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.bionlp-1.61.pdf), with additional work under review at *Neurocomputing* and at *Computer Speech & Language*. Previously, I interned at **IBM Research** (LLM training efficiency) and **CNeRG Lab, IIT Kharagpur** (NLP / efficient fine-tuning). I graduated from **IIT Patna** in 2025.

Outside research, I've led teams to top finishes at the **Inter-IIT Tech Meet** and **Amazon ML Challenge 2024 (3rd)**, founded the **Research Club at IIT Patna**, and headed the **Machine Learning Club** at NJack. I also play competitive chess (state-level), do theatre, and trek in the Himalayas.

Feel free to [drop me a line](mailto:atharva1763@gmail.com) — I'm always happy to chat about LLM systems, sustainability in AI, or research collaborations.
